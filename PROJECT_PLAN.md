# AI-Powered Air Quality Intelligence & Sustainable Action Assistant

## Primary SDG
SDG 11 – Sustainable Cities and Communities

## Supporting SDGs
SDG 13 – Climate Action
SDG 3 – Good Health and Well-being

## Problem Statement

People often receive air-quality information as numbers such as
AQI, PM2.5 and PM10 but may not understand what these values mean,
how air quality is changing, or what sustainable actions can help
reduce pollution.

## Proposed Solution

An AI-powered system that analyzes air-quality data, explains
pollution levels in simple language, identifies trends, answers
air-quality questions using trusted sources, and recommends
sustainable actions.

## Target Users

- Students
- Campus communities
- Citizens
- Environmental awareness groups

## Main Features

1. AQI Dashboard
2. Pollutant Analysis
3. AI Air Quality Explanation
4. Pollution Trend Analysis
5. Sustainable Action Recommendations
6. RAG-based Air Quality Assistant
7. Optional AQI Prediction

## AI Components

- Classification
- Pattern Detection
- RAG
- Summarization
- Conversational AI
- Recommendation
- Optional Prediction

## Responsible AI

- Use trusted sources
- Explain AI-generated results
- Avoid medical diagnosis
- Protect user privacy
- Clearly communicate uncertainty
- Do not claim pollution sources without evidence
